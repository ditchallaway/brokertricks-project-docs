### **User Journey Steps**

#### **1\. User Sign-Up and Onboarding**

* **User Action**: A new real estate agent signs up on your WordPress site.  
* **System Action**:  
  * User details (name, brokerage, team affiliation, role) are collected.  
  * **Tags Applied in Fluent CRM**:  
    * "New User," "Agent" (or "Broker"), and a tag for their specific brokerage (e.g., "Coldwell Banker").  
* **Automated Email**: A welcome email is sent with basic onboarding info, outlining the benefits of their personalized store and encouraging them to explore.

#### **2\. Initial Profile Setup**

* **User Action**: The agent logs into their dashboard to complete their profile (e.g., phone number, DBA, and any other necessary branding info).  
* **System Action**:  
  * The collected profile info populates their Printify store products automatically, customizing mockups with their branding.  
  * Fluent CRM tags are updated to include "Profile Completed."  
* **Automated Email**: A "Profile Complete" email is sent, confirming that their store is ready and encouraging them to browse products. Include a personalized link to their store.

#### **3\. Product Browsing and Engagement**

* **User Action**: The agent explores their custom Printify store and views or adds items to their cart.  
* **System Action**:  
  * SureCart updates Fluent CRM with tags based on browsing activity (e.g., "Viewed Apparel," "Viewed Promo Items").  
  * If they add items to their cart but don’t complete the purchase, a "Cart Abandoned" tag is applied.  
* **Automated Email**: If tagged as "Cart Abandoned," send a friendly reminder email within 24 hours, reminding them of the items in their cart and including a call to action to complete the purchase. Include images of the abandoned items for personalization.

#### **4\. Product Purchase**

* **User Action**: The agent completes a purchase on SureCart.  
* **System Action**:  
  * Tags such as "Purchased Product" and the specific product category are added in Fluent CRM.  
  * Fluent CRM updates the user's tag to "Active Customer."  
* **Automated Email**:  
  * Send an order confirmation email with a summary of the purchased items and branding details.  
  * Include information on when they can expect shipping updates.

#### **5\. Shipping and Delivery Updates**

* **User Action**: The agent waits for their order to arrive.  
* **System Action**:  
  * SureCart or Printify sends shipping notifications, and Fluent CRM tags are updated accordingly (e.g., "Shipped," "Out for Delivery").  
* **Automated Email**: Trigger emails for each shipping stage, ensuring the user stays informed. Use shipping tags to trigger these updates, so the process is seamless.

#### **6\. Post-Purchase Follow-Up and Feedback**

* **User Action**: The agent receives their order and uses the items.  
* **System Action**:  
  * Fluent CRM tags the user with "Order Received" approximately 3-5 days post-delivery.  
* **Automated Email**: Send a follow-up email inviting feedback on their purchase experience and satisfaction with the products. Offer a link to leave a review or provide feedback directly through their dashboard.  
  * **Optional**: Include a special offer or discount for a repeat purchase, especially if they haven’t interacted with their store in a while (re-engagement strategy).

#### **7\. Ongoing Engagement and Product Recommendations**

* **User Action**: The agent may continue to browse or purchase additional items.  
* **System Action**:  
  * Fluent CRM monitors user activity and applies tags based on repeated behaviors or lack of engagement.  
* **Automated Email**:  
  * Based on user tags, periodically send relevant content such as “New Products Available” or seasonal promotional items.  
  * Re-engagement emails can be triggered if the user hasn’t interacted for a set time, offering discounts or highlighting popular products.

### **Business Model Canvas**

**1\. Customer Segments**

* **Primary**: Real estate agents and brokers in the U.S. with undeveloped lot or land listings.  
* **Secondary**: Independent real estate agents looking for personalized, branded promotional materials.

**2\. Value Propositions**

* **Personalized Product Experience**: Real estate agents access a pre-populated, customized store that aligns with their brokerage’s brand identity (e.g., logo, colors, style).  
* **Seamless, Ready-to-Order Process**: No need for users to manually upload or configure brand elements—every product is customized with their personal and brand information.

**3\. Channels**

* **Website/Dashboard**: Primary interface for user onboarding, browsing products, and ordering.  
* **Email Marketing (Fluent CRM)**: Automated onboarding, abandoned cart follow-ups, order confirmations, and engagement emails.  
* **WordPress Dashboard**: Accessible to users as a branded, personal hub.  
* **Social Media**: Targeted advertising for brand visibility among real estate professionals.

**4\. Customer Relationships**

* **Self-Service**: Users handle profile setup and ordering through an intuitive dashboard.  
* **Automated Engagement**: Fluent CRM-driven emails for onboarding, reminders, and follow-ups create an automated, consistent customer experience.  
* **Personalized Customer Experience**: Custom dashboards pre-populated with agent-specific branding create a sense of exclusivity and ease of use.  
* **Feedback Loop**: Post-purchase emails and surveys to gather feedback and continuously improve offerings.

**5\. Revenue Streams**

* **Product Sales**: Margin on each Printify order made through SureCart, with pricing tiers (Basic, Bundled, Premium) for customization levels.  
* **Premium Package Upgrades**: Offers for larger brokerages and multi-agent packages.  
* **One-Time Set-Up Fee** (Optional): For personalized onboarding services or additional customization requests.  
* **Re-Engagement Offers**: Discounts for returning customers, creating recurring revenue from agents ordering new seasonal items or marketing materials.

**6\. Key Resources**

* **Technical Resources**: WordPress platform, SureCart, SureTriggers, Printify integration, Fluent CRM for email automation, and custom dashboard configuration.  
* **Brand Data**: Style guides, logos, and color palettes for major real estate brands to ensure compliance.  
* **Marketing Assets**: Templates, email sequences, social media assets, and promotional materials for customer acquisition and retention.

**7\. Key Activities**

* **Onboarding & Customization**: Streamlining the initial setup for each agent’s store, gathering key details, and automating profile setup.  
* **API & Webhook Management**: Automating product synchronization between Printify and SureCart, updating product catalogs, and ensuring order fulfillment.  
* **Customer Engagement**: Using Fluent CRM tags and automations to provide timely follow-ups, abandoned cart emails, and re-engagement offers.  
* **Order Fulfillment**: Managing and overseeing the Printify integration for seamless order processing and shipment tracking.

**8\. Key Partnerships**

* **Printify**: Print-on-demand platform to handle product printing, customization, and shipping.  
* **SureCart & SureTriggers**: Facilitates e-commerce transactions and automation within the WordPress ecosystem.  
* **WordPress and Fluent CRM**: Central to user management, email marketing, and customer relationship workflows.
