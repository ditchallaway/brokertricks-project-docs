

# **Real Estate Marketing Value Proposition Canvas**

Purpose:

Explain the methods and marketing principles that inform what we highlight in our listing images. Show how data drives our design choices and help agents appreciate our infographics and image assets, demonstrate expertise in compliant, strategic real estate marketing.

The problem: the value proposition canvas is a tool to help get to the crux of a buyers motivation. The traditional canvas requires a buyer persona. For real estate, Marketing to a specific buyer persona exposed agents to equal housing violations. Our canvas solves that problem.

### 1\. Property Overview

* Property Type: Residential, land, commercial, mixed-use, etc.  
* Key Attributes: Size, layout, condition, zoning, amenities, unique features.  
* Location Highlights: Neighborhood, accessibility, nearby schools/amenities.

Note: Facts only; no statements implying buyer preference.

Example Description:

*"This 3-bedroom single-family home sits on a 0.25-acre lot with a renovated kitchen, attached garage, and walking distance to local parks and transit."*

### 2\. Target Buyer Jobs

Definition: Functional purposes the property fulfills (what buyers are trying to accomplish).

Examples:

* Family residence  
* Investment property  
* Commercial or mixed-use development  
* Recreational or agricultural use

Note: Jobs are functional; no assumptions about buyer demographics or lifestyle.

### 3\. Buyer Pains Addressed

Definition: Challenges or obstacles the property helps overcome.

Examples:

* Limited inventory of a certain property type in this area.  
* Difficulty finding properties with specific amenities/features.  
* Complex purchase or development constraints in the neighborhood.

Note: Focus on functional pain points, not buyer identity.

### 4\. Buyer Gains Delivered

Definition: Positive outcomes or advantages the property provides.

Examples:

* Investment potential or rental income  
* Turnkey condition or low-maintenance lot  
* Unique features that enhance lifestyle or functionality

Note: Gains emphasize property value; avoid implying lifestyle or family-type preferences.

### 5\. Highlighted Marketing Features

Purpose: Explain why specific imagery or assets were created for this property.

Examples:

* Aerial shots highlighting lot size or location advantages  
* Interior photos showing recent renovations or key rooms  
* Interactive maps emphasizing proximity to amenities  
* Property composites with visualized potential (Photopea layers)

Tip for Agents: Helps them understand why these assets are effective for the identified buyer jobs.

### 6\. Marketing Insights from Comps

Definition: Data-driven observations from similar properties sold nearby.

Examples:

* Features that historically drove interest or offers  
* Visual angles and formats that captured attention  
* Pricing or presentation strategies that worked

Tip for Agents: Encourages appreciation of the research underpinning the marketing assets.

### 7\. Compliance & Messaging Notes

* All language and visuals are Equal Housing compliant.  
* Focus on functional benefits, not buyer demographics.  
* Demonstrates professional respect for the regulation while showcasing property potential.

Tip for Agents: Highlights your expertise in both marketing and ethical compliance.

## How to Use This Canvas

* Copy-paste the example property description to start.  
* Identify buyer jobs, pains, and gains using comps and market insights.  
* Map marketing features to the buyer jobs they address.  
* Include notes on compliance to reassure agents and clients.  
* Share internally or publicly to educate agents about your asset strategy.
